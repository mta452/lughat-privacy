Lughat Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

App: Lughat – English to Urdu Dictionary

Developer: Muhammad Tayyab Akram ("we", "us")

Contact: tayyab.akram@proton.me

This policy explains how the Lughat app ("the app") handles information. In short: the current version of Lughat does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or sensitive user data.

1. Information we collect

None. Lughat has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no backend servers. We do not collect, receive, or have access to your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers (such as advertising ID), or any other personal or sensitive data.

2. Network access and permissions

The release version of Lughat does not request any Android permissions, including the Internet permission. The dictionary is bundled inside the app and works fully offline. The app does not use analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting services, and contains no third-party SDK that sends data off your device.

3. Data stored on your device

To work properly, the app stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Recent searches: the last words you opened (up to 20), shown on the home screen so you can return to them quickly.
  • Settings: your preferences, such as theme and Urdu text size.
  • Dictionary file: a copy of the bundled dictionary.

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. The app is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so this data is not copied to Google or to other devices through backup.

Deleting your data: you can remove recent searches at any time with Settings → Clear recent searches. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all data the app has stored.

4. Copy and Share

If you tap Copy or Share on a word, the app places that word's text on the system clipboard or opens the Android share sheet. What happens next is controlled by you and the app you choose to share with; Lughat does not see or receive that content, and the policies of the receiving app apply.

5. Links opened from other apps

Lughat can open a word when it receives a link of the form lughat://word/<word>. The word is used only to look up an entry in the on-device dictionary. It is not sent anywhere and is not loaded as a web address.

6. Sharing of data

We do not sell, share, rent, or disclose any user data to third parties, because we do not collect any.

7. Data retention

We hold no user data. Data stored on your device remains until you clear it or uninstall the app (see section 3).

8. Children

Lughat is a general-audience reference tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

9. Security

All data stays on your device. The app does not use the network, which removes the risk of data being intercepted in transit. The bundled dictionary is stored in encrypted form.

10. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Lughat changes how data is handled (for example, by adding advertising or analytics), we will update this policy and the Data safety section of the Google Play listing before or at the same time as that version is released. The effective date above shows when this policy was last updated.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: tayyab.akram@proton.me
